# How to Use Abe (plain-English guide)

Abe is your ministry content partner. You **talk to him like a person** — in
plain English — and he talks back, drafts, and revises. No commands to memorize.

---

## Reaching Abe

Come back to this project (the `lift-faith-website` workspace in Claude Code).
Then either:

- **Type `/abe`**, or
- **Just say his name** — e.g. *"Abe, write me a podcast on the Prodigal Son."*

Either way, once he's in, you just keep talking.

---

## The rhythm: ask → draft → react → revise

You don't have to get it right in one sentence. Go back and forth:

> **You:** Abe, I want an episode on worry — Matthew 6.
> **Abe:** *(writes a full Under the Scope script)* Want it longer or tighter?
> **You:** Tighter, and make the cold open hit harder.
> **Abe:** *(revises)* Done — trimmed 4 minutes and reworked the open.
> **You:** Perfect. Now 3 Shorts and 5 YouTube titles.
> **Abe:** *(delivers them)*

Say things like: "shorter," "that's not my voice," "more personal," "give me 3
more options," "add a cross-reference," "make it for Facebook."

---

## Things you can ask Abe for

- "Turn this sermon/podcast into the full set." (outline + YouTube + Shorts +
  social posts + cross-references, all at once)
- "Write a podcast / sermon outline / Bible study on ___."
- "Give me 3 Shorts and 5 YouTube titles from last week's episode."
- "Draft Facebook, X, Substack, and LinkedIn posts for this message."
- "Suggest cross-references for ___."
- "Make this sound more like me."

---

## What Abe remembers

- **Your voice** — saved in `abe/voice-profile.md`. Abe reads it every time, so he
  always writes as Pastor Billy Daws (NKJV, no clichés, "under the scope"). The
  more you add — especially a transcript of your own preaching — the closer he
  sounds to you.
- **His output** — everything he writes is saved in `abe/output/` so you can find
  it later.

Within one conversation Abe remembers everything you've said. Across separate
conversations, the **voice profile is his long-term memory** — that's why it's a
file you can keep improving.

---

## Making Abe better over time

When you edit something Abe wrote, tell him what you changed and why ("I'd never
say 'journey,'" "I always open with a question"). He'll fold it into your voice
profile so every future draft inherits it. Abe gets more *you* every week.
