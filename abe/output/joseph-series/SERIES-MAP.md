# Series Map — "The Unseen Hand: Joseph Under the Scope"

**Show:** Under the Scope with Pastor Billy Daws
**Series:** The life of Joseph — Genesis 37–50
**Episodes:** 6
**Series anchor verse (all NKJV):** Genesis 50:20 —
> "But as for you, you meant evil against me; but God meant it for good, in
> order to bring it about as it is this day, to save many people alive."

**Doctrinal frame:** Conservative Southern Baptist; high view of Scripture as
authoritative and inerrant. Joseph's story is read as real history and as a
window into God's providence — His unseen hand working through real sin, real
suffering, and real waiting to accomplish real good. We teach the theme of
providence humbly, letting the text carry it, and we save the full weight of
Genesis 50:20 for the finish while foreshadowing it from Episode 1 on.

**How the arc builds:** The dreams and the coat (37) → sold into slavery, then
into Potiphar's house and the temptation (39) → prison and the forgotten dream
(39–40) → Pharaoh's dream and the elevation (41) → the brothers come to Egypt,
the testing and the reveal (42–45) → reunion, Jacob to Egypt, and the payoff:
"you meant it for evil, but God meant it for good" (45–50).

## Episode 1 — "The Dreamer and the Coat"

- **Alternate titles:** "When Your Own Family Turns on You" · "The Pit Before the
  Palace"
- **Passage(s):** Genesis 37 (NKJV)
- **Premise:** A favored son with a God-given dream is hated, stripped, and sold
  by his own brothers — and the very moment his life looks finished is the moment
  God's unseen hand starts moving.
- **Anchor verse:** Genesis 37:20 —
  > "Come therefore, let us now kill him and cast him into some pit; and we shall
  > say, 'Some wild beast has devoured him.' We shall see what will become of his
  > dreams!"
  *(Dramatic irony: the brothers mock the dreams — and set them in motion.)*
- **Supporting / bridge verses:**
  - Genesis 37:3–4 (the coat, the favoritism, the hatred)
  - Genesis 37:11 ("his father kept the matter in mind")
  - Romans 8:28 (foreshadow the theme — all things work together for good)
- **Takeaway:** When life throws you in the pit, you're not out of God's story —
  you're in the part where His hand moves in the dark. The dream isn't dead; it's
  underground.

---

## Episode 2 — "The Lord Was With Him: Slave, and Faithful"

- **Alternate titles:** "Faithful in Someone Else's House" · "The Temptation and
  the Runaway Coat"
- **Passage(s):** Genesis 39 (NKJV)
- **Premise:** Sold into Potiphar's house, Joseph doesn't sulk — he serves; and
  when temptation comes to buy his integrity, he'd rather lose a coat and land in
  prison than sin against God.
- **Anchor verse:** Genesis 39:9 —
  > "There is no one greater in this house than I, nor has he kept back anything
  > from me but you, because you are his wife. How then can I do this great
  > wickedness, and sin against God?"
- **Supporting / bridge verses:**
  - Genesis 39:2 ("The LORD was with Joseph, and he was a successful man")
  - Genesis 39:21 ("the LORD was with Joseph and showed him mercy")
  - Genesis 39:12 (he left the garment and fled)
- **Takeaway:** Success isn't a place; it's the presence of God. "The LORD was
  with him" is true in the palace and in the prison — and it's true of you where
  you are right now. Do the right thing even when it costs you.

---

## Episode 3 — "Forgotten, But Not Forsaken"

- **Alternate titles:** "The Prison and the Long Wait" · "When the Man Who
  Promised Forgets You"
- **Passage(s):** Genesis 39:20–40:23 (NKJV)
- **Premise:** In prison Joseph serves two forgotten dreamers, tells them the
  truth from God, asks to be remembered — and the man he helped walks free and
  forgets him for two more years.
- **Anchor verse:** Genesis 40:23 —
  > "Yet the chief butler did not remember Joseph, but forgot him."
- **Supporting / bridge verses:**
  - Genesis 40:8 ("Do not interpretations belong to God?")
  - Genesis 39:21–23 (the LORD with Joseph in prison, the keeper trusts him)
  - Psalm 105:18–19 ("They hurt his feet with fetters... the word of the LORD
    tested him")
- **Takeaway:** People forget you; God never does. The waiting room isn't wasted
  time — it's where God tests the word and shapes the man. Keep being faithful in
  the place where nobody's watching.

---

## Episode 4 — "From the Pit to the Palace"

- **Alternate titles:** "It Is Not in Me: God Will Answer" · "Two Years Later,
  Suddenly"
- **Passage(s):** Genesis 41 (NKJV)
- **Premise:** Pharaoh dreams, the cupbearer finally remembers, and in a single
  day Joseph goes from the dungeon to second-in-command over Egypt — because he
  gave God the credit instead of taking it.
- **Anchor verse:** Genesis 41:16 —
  > "So Joseph answered Pharaoh, saying, 'It is not in me; God will give Pharaoh
  > an answer of peace.'"
- **Supporting / bridge verses:**
  - Genesis 41:38 ("a man in whom is the Spirit of God")
  - Genesis 41:39–40 (Pharaoh sets him over all Egypt)
  - Genesis 41:51–52 (Manasseh — "God has made me forget"; Ephraim — "God has
    caused me to be fruitful in the land of my affliction")
- **Takeaway:** God's promotions come on God's clock, and they come to the humble.
  Thirteen years of pit and prison were the training for the palace. Give God the
  glory in the small rooms and trust Him with the timing of the big ones.

---

## Episode 5 — "The Reckoning and the Reveal"

- **Alternate titles:** "God Sent Me Before You" · "The Brothers Come Back"
- **Passage(s):** Genesis 42–45 (NKJV)
- **Premise:** The brothers who sold Joseph bow before him without knowing it; he
  tests their hearts, sees them changed, and then — through tears — reveals
  himself and reframes the whole story around the hand of God.
- **Anchor verse:** Genesis 45:5 —
  > "But now, do not therefore be grieved or angry with yourselves because you
  > sold me here; for God sent me before you to preserve life."
- **Supporting / bridge verses:**
  - Genesis 42:21 ("We are truly guilty concerning our brother")
  - Genesis 44:33 (Judah offers himself in the boy's place)
  - Genesis 45:7–8 ("it was not you who sent me here, but God")
- **Takeaway:** Real forgiveness sees God's hand above the hands that hurt you.
  Joseph doesn't excuse the sin — he traces it up to a sovereign God who used it
  to preserve life. That's how the wounded set the guilty free.

---

## Episode 6 — "You Meant It for Evil, God Meant It for Good"

- **Alternate titles:** "The Reunion and the Reason" · "Genesis 50:20 Under the
  Scope"
- **Passage(s):** Genesis 45:25–50:26 (NKJV)
- **Premise:** Jacob learns his son is alive, the family is reunited in Egypt, and
  after their father dies the brothers fear revenge — until Joseph says the
  sentence the whole series has been building toward.
- **Anchor verse:** Genesis 50:20 —
  > "But as for you, you meant evil against me; but God meant it for good, in
  > order to bring it about as it is this day, to save many people alive."
- **Supporting / bridge verses:**
  - Genesis 45:28 ("It is enough. Joseph my son is still alive")
  - Genesis 46:4 ("I will go down with you to Egypt... and Joseph will put his
    hand on your eyes")
  - Genesis 50:21 ("do not be afraid; I will provide for you... And he comforted
    them and spoke kindly to them")
- **Takeaway:** The same event can carry two meanings at once — evil in the hands
  of men, good in the hands of God. Nothing done to you is wasted in His economy.
  That's not a slogan; it's the whole life of Joseph, and it's the promise you can
  stand on.

---

## Series arc in one sentence

From a favored boy in a coat to a ruler over Egypt, God's unseen hand works
through betrayal, slavery, temptation, and prison to bring a whole family — and
a whole nation — through, so that what men meant for evil, God meant for good.
