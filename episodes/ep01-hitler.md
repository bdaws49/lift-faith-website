## 📋 THE VERDICT

- **What the church got right:** A faithful remnant refused to bow. The Confessing Church and the 1934 Barmen Declaration confessed Christ alone as Lord over the church. Bonhoeffer, Niemöller, and Karl Barth resisted — some to death.
- **Where the church failed:** The majority — the "German Christians" — fused cross and swastika, adopted the Aryan Paragraph (expelling clergy of Jewish descent), installed a Reich Bishop, and baptized Nazi nationalism as Christian. The wider church largely accommodated, and stayed silent through Kristallnacht and the Holocaust.
- **What faithful believers did:** Signed the Barmen Declaration. Preached against the regime (Niemöller imprisoned 1938–45). Bonhoeffer joined the resistance and was executed in 1945. The White Rose students resisted; Bishop von Galen denounced the Nazi euthanasia program.
- **What we must not repeat:** Never wed the gospel to a political movement or nation. Never trade prophetic witness for state approval or cultural power. Never let nationalism wear the name of Christ. Never stay silent while the vulnerable are targeted.
- **Three Scriptures:** Acts 5:29 ("We must obey God rather than men") · Isaiah 5:20 ("Woe to those who call evil good") · Proverbs 31:8–9 ("Speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves")
- **One action:** This week, name one place where your national or political loyalty has slipped ahead of your loyalty to Christ — and repent of it. Then speak up once for someone your culture treats as expendable.
