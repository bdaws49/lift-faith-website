### Key people, events & documents referenced
- **Adolf Hitler** appointed Chancellor of Germany — January 30, 1933
- **The "German Christians" (Deutsche Christen)** — pro-Nazi church movement; won roughly **two-thirds of the July 1933 German church elections**
- **Ludwig Müller** — installed as **Reich Bishop (Reichsbischof)**, 1933
- **The Aryan Paragraph** applied to the church (barring clergy of Jewish descent), 1933
- **The Confessing Church (Bekennende Kirche)** and the **Barmen Declaration**, May 1934
- **Martin Niemöller** — founder of the Pastors' Emergency League; imprisoned 1938–1945; author of "First they came…"
- **Dietrich Bonhoeffer** — led the **Finkenwalde** seminary; executed at **Flossenbürg** concentration camp, **April 9, 1945**
- **Elisabeth Schmitz** — memorandum *On the Situation of the German Non-Aryans* (1935–36)
- **August Froehlich** — Catholic priest who protested the abuse of Polish forced laborers; died at **Dachau**, 1942
- **Paul Schneider** — "the Preacher of Buchenwald"; died 1939

### Primary sources
- **The Theological Declaration of Barmen** (1934) — full text widely published
- ***Mit brennender Sorge*** (1937) — papal encyclical read aloud in German Catholic churches
- **Martin Niemöller** — sermons and postwar statements
